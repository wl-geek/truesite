# TrueSite audit and testing: 17 Oct 2026

## Scope and method

This is a read-only audit of `main` at `4bc3d28` (= `origin/main`), taken on submission day. It follows the 15 Oct audit (`docs/audit-2026-10-15.md`) and the 16 Oct re-audit (`docs/audit-2026-10-16.md`). The product it checks is the one set by Wai's Mac session in PRs #71–#106, not the older text in `docs/BRIEF.md`.

- **Area audits.** Three auditors read the code and verified each finding themselves with traces, scratch scripts or throwaway probes:
  - **AR:** live AR, pose, levelling, look-around, Quick Look
  - **Check:** the virtual demo, the check pipeline, Gemini, the report, issues and the server routes
  - **Ops:** deploy, BIM data, samples, eval, tests and docs
- **Pages and UX.** An earlier audit of the start page, routing, the viewer and the phone layout ran at `85032e4`, before #103–#106. Its four mediums were re-checked in the current code. None of the files it names changed after `85032e4`, apart from `web/src/api/client.ts` (C-26, re-checked). Its lows are marked unverified.
- **Testing.** Each tool's results are in three tests-only PRs, all open and none merged:
  - **Mutation testing:** StrykerJS on the web code (#108) and cosmic-ray on `validate.py` and `severity.py` (#110)
  - **Property tests and fuzzing:** Hypothesis property tests and a live Hypothesis client, plus Schemathesis API fuzzing (#110)
  - **End-to-end:** Playwright on desktop Chrome, iPad Pro 11 and iPhone 15 profiles, with a live AR test fed by synthetic camera videos rendered from the model (#109)
  - **Security scans:** bandit, pip-audit and npm audit (#110)
- **Merging.** When several sources reported the same root cause, they became one finding, which keeps the highest severity the evidence supports. The 45 reported findings became 41. Three recheck-only items (#48, #64, #100) were folded into C-26, C-18 and C-05. Every finding from testing has a repro.

#### C-01 · D1 desk sheet says "about 1 m"; its 7 cm code can't be read or reach Good there

**Status:** Open

- **Severity:** Medium · **Area:** AR
- **Location:** `web/src/ui/LivePage.tsx:552`; also `LivePage.tsx:426`, `web/src/scene/arMath.ts:69-72` (`coachHint`), `web/src/ui/QualityBadge.tsx:17`, `bim/models/site/anchors.json` (D1 `qr_size_m` 0.07)
- **Evidence:**
  - Four places give the same advice, all written for room1's 12 cm D1:
    - the desk steps: "Point at the code from about 1 m away, then step back slowly."
    - the coach, while provisional: "Move within about 1 m of the code, then step back slowly."
    - the 5 s tip: "…move within about 1 m…"
    - the badge: "Start within about 1 m of the code, then step back"
  - Site D1, the start page's quickest way to try it, has a 7 cm code.
  - `grabFrame` scales every detection frame to 640 px wide (`camera.ts:91`, `overlay.ts:29`), so a landscape iPad detects on 640×360.
    - At 1 m the 33-module code is about 37 px, 1.1 px per module.
    - In video pixels it is about 110 px, under the 120 px "good" threshold in either orientation.
  - Scratch decode (`d1decode.mjs`):
    - Setup: the real D1 payload, ECL M, the repo's `synthQr` renderer at the overlay's detection size, and zxing-wasm with `ZXING_OPTIONS`.
    - Landscape fails at 1 m and decodes at 0.8 m and closer; portrait decodes at 1 m (65 px).
  - Scratch pose run (`d1pose.mjs`, about 0.4 px detection noise, 30° view):
    - At 1 m, 0% of frames are good and trusted, and 27% (landscape) or 10% (portrait) are ambiguous.
    - At 0.6 m, 100% are good and trusted.
